## Contracts behind the accepted checkpoint

- [Support bundle](SUPPORT_BUNDLE.md): bounded, inspectable, redacted local export with an explicit home; no provider call, service startup or upload. Native usage remains unknown where accounting is unavailable.
- [Reviewed candidate staging](REVIEWED_CANDIDATE_STAGING.md): exact source/runtime/evidence pins, notice inclusion and retained process ownership. Inclusion preserves the dated notices; it does not settle remaining legal or binary applicability questions or retrofit beta184/185.
- [Package verification limits](PACKAGE_VERIFICATION_LIMITS.md): finite bytes, inventory and paths for read-only integrity checks. Checksums bind supplied bytes; they do not authenticate the publisher or provide a hostile-writer filesystem sandbox.
- [Terminal qualification](TERMINAL_QUALIFICATION.md): a declared Node/ICU/Unicode measurement model and owned PTY specimens, with explicit limits for other terminals and platforms.

## Work batches and review availability

Estimated **3–4 hour batches** describe coherent work and review units within stages. They are not launch deadlines, minimum run times, forced durations or guaranteed unattended completion. Success is useful checked work and accepted exit evidence; elapsed time, tokens and prompt count are not product outcomes.

App review is pulled by an **active native coding conversation**. An inactive or disconnected app can leave a durable review wait; it cannot be promised to wake its own chat or silently switch to API review. Only an explicitly authorized API-review policy has its documented automatic behavior, within its mode and original limits. See [host review compatibility](HOST_COMPATIBILITY.md#review-and-lifecycle) and [workflow roles](PRODUCT_CONTEXT.md#roles-and-modes).

This static roadmap is a dated snapshot, not a background supervisor. Selecting a stage, reading a guide or choosing a workflow grants no new execution scope, provider consent, spending, native permissions or publication authority. Approved context sent to remote workers or app reviewers may leave the Mac; keep credentials, endpoints, personal paths and raw task evidence private. Changes still require exact approval and preserve deadlines, spending and uncertain ownership.
