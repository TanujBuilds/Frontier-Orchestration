# Independent whole-report research acceptance

This is the fixed semantic acceptance rubric for the proposed bounded validation. Hash these exact UTF-8 bytes in the explicitly approved policy. It does not authorize dispatch or declare any report acceptable.

An independent reviewer compares the whole candidate with its exact selected evidence and source snapshot. Provenance, offered context, claim IDs, inference labels and model approval flags never establish semantic support.

Acceptance requires all of the following:

1. At least five useful, supported material facts spanning the requested families. An empty or trivial report cannot satisfy coverage.
2. Every material factual premise in claims, comparisons and recommendations is supported as cited for the actual subject, alternative and configuration. Evidence about one alternative cannot establish another's properties.
3. Antecedents, conditions, negation and configuration limits survive quotation and synthesis. Nearby unselected context cannot support an additional premise. A repair must explicitly select it or narrow the assertion.
4. A useful conditional recommendation follows from supported premises and explicit assumptions. Untested design proposals and unknowns are clearly labelled. Benchmarks and an implemented queue are not required merely to state a conditional proposal.
5. Local durability, failure visibility, replay or idempotency mechanisms do not imply exactly-once external effects or permission for blind retry. Application/provider reconciliation premises require their own evidence or explicit gaps.
6. The review covers every material unit in the replacement, including new errors introduced by correction. A previous or delta-only acceptance cannot cover changed prose, sources, offers or rubric.

Return accept only with full unit coverage and no unresolved findings. Otherwise return focused findings bound to the exact candidate and rubric: unit, issue code, rationale, and optional actually offered selectors. The writer makes corrections; the reviewer does not rewrite official citations or report prose. A second negative review ends the bounded attempt unaccepted.
